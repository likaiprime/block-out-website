Block Out! Level 16 — Video & Tipps

An introductory puzzle that teaches you the basic sliding mechanics. Take your time to understand how blocks move through the gates.

Level 16 wird im Level 11-20 Batch walkthrough behandelt — scrollen Sie durch das Video, um Ihr Level zu finden.

Tipps für dieses Level

  1. 1

    Identify each block's color and find the matching gate before you move anything.

  2. 2

    Move smaller blocks first to free up space for larger pieces.

  3. 3

    Watch for blocks that share an exit — those usually need to leave in a specific order.

  4. 4

    If you get stuck, undo and retrace the gate sequence from the bottom up.
