Block Out! Livello 145 — Video guida e consigli

An advanced level with overlapping pieces and complex routing. Solving it cleanly often requires moving blocks out of the way and back.

Livello precedente 144Tutti i livelliLivello successivo 146

Consigli per questo livello

  1. 1

    Identify the choke point on the board and treat it as your bottleneck — sequence around it.

  2. 2

    Some blocks must be moved twice: once to clear a path, then back to their gate.

  3. 3

    Watch the video at 0.5x speed to catch micro-moves that look like one slide.

  4. 4

    If two pieces have only one viable exit each, there's usually only one correct order.

  5. 5

    Reset early if your first 4 moves don't match the optimal opening — you'll save time.
