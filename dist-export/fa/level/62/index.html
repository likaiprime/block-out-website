Block Out! Level 62 — راهنمای ویدئویی & نکات

A challenging level with multiple block types and tight space. Wrong moves can box you in — visualize the full sequence first.

سطح 62 در راهنمایی بسته 60-63 پوشش داده شده است — از ویدئو برای پیدا کردن سطح خود جلوگیری کنید.

نکات برای این سطح

  1. 1

    Map out the full evacuation order for every gate before your first move.

  2. 2

    Long pieces need a clear runway — clear horizontal or vertical space first.

  3. 3

    If a block can only exit through one path, leave that path empty until the end.

  4. 4

    Don't be afraid to backtrack a block one or two squares to unblock another.

  5. 5

    Save the 'pivot' piece — the one in the densest corner — for last.
