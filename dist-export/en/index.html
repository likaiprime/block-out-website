How it works

How to play Block Out!

Three rules. Then it's all about the order.

  1. 1
    Step01

    Slide blocks toward matching gates

    Each colored block needs to leave through the gate of its own color. Drag the block in any direction; it slides until it hits an obstacle or the wall.

  2. 2
    Step02

    Plan the order before you move

    The wrong block first will block the others. Look at the gates and figure out which piece can leave last — work backward from there.

  3. 3
    Step03

    Use undo, then commit

    Undo is unlimited and free. Use it to test a sequence, but once you know the order, play it cleanly — the satisfaction is in the solve, not the trial-and-error.
