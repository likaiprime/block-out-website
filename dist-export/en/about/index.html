About this site

An independent walkthrough resource for Block Out! by Grand Games.

What this site is

blockout.cc is an independent, fan-made walkthrough site. It exists for one reason: to give Block Out! players a clean answer when they're stuck on a level — no upsells, no hint-coin paywall, no scrolling past five ads to find a 30-second video.

About the game

Block Out! is a color-sort puzzle by Grand Games for iOS and Android. You slide colored blocks across a board so each block exits through the gate of its matching color. Levels start as gentle introductions and ramp into multi-step planning puzzles where the order of moves is everything.

What you'll find here

  • A 16:9 video walkthrough for every level we cover
  • Difficulty rating and a written description per level
  • 3-5 written tips that explain the why, not just the how
  • An FAQ for common questions on each level page
  • Quick prev/next navigation and a sticky group selector

Suggested workflow

  • Watch the video once at full speed to see the solution shape
  • Read the tips before your second attempt — they explain the constraint
  • If you're still stuck, slow the video to 0.5x for the first 5 moves

Why we built this

  • Most existing guides are crammed with ads and 30-second videos behind 2-minute intros
  • Tips on other sites tell you to 'plan ahead' without explaining what to look for
  • We wanted a guide that loads fast on a phone and respects your time
